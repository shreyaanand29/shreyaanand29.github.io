Generative vector tracing

Classic image tracing fits curves to pixels and produces paths no human would draw.

Project Vectormitate: a generative model of vector strokes, so traced output is editable, intentional geometry. Then a second method snaps those traces to the shapes the designer meant. Two patents.

  • Advanced Image Tracing for Vectorizing Raster Images ↗ US 2026/0024212. Filed July 2024, published January 2026.
  • Aligning Traces to Geometric Shapes ↗ US 2026/0057577. Filed August 2024, published February 2026.
