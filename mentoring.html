Mentoring

Most of what I know about switching roles I learned the slow way.

Three companies in three years: a creative tool at Adobe, a platform team at LinkedIn, payments at Airbnb. Every switch taught me something I wish someone had just told me. So now I tell people.

01How it works

Interviews, offers, or a resume that undersells you. Book a session.

Bring a specific question. "Review my resume for this role" beats "any advice?" every time.

  • 1:1 sessionBook a slot. 30 or 45 minutes.→
  • Resume reviewBook on Topmate with the role you are targeting. You get line-level notes back.→
  • ReferralsFor roles I can genuinely speak to. Message me on LinkedIn with the posting.→
